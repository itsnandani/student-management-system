About Scholar Portal

A modern, browser-based student administration workspace

Scholar Student Management Portal is a frontend-only application built with HTML5, CSS3 and vanilla JavaScript. Students, attendance, courses, examinations, payments and preferences are stored locally in your browser.

No backend or external service is required. Export a backup from Settings to move data between devices. The original student CRUD, validation, search, sorting and reporting workflows are preserved and extended.

Privacy note: portal data stays in this browser's LocalStorage unless you explicitly export it.
